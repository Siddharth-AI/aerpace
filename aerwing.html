Why it exists

A wing that takes off like a helicopter, flies on hydrogen, and lands where people already are.

aerWing is the aircraft at the centre of aerVerse. It flies between aerDock hubs, carries people, care or cargo, and runs on hydrogen made at the docks with aerVolt solar.

Technology

Five systems

What makes itfly.

Vertical take-off

No runway. aerWing lifts straight up from the pad and lands the same way, so a dock fits where an airport never could.

Hydrogen energy

aerVolt solar powers the dock, the dock produces green hydrogen, and the hydrogen powers the aircraft.

Electric propulsion

Ducted electric fans set into the wings and the tail. The ducts keep the blades enclosed.

Smart systems

Sensors read the airspace around the aircraft, and aerOS keeps it in step with the docks and the network.

One wing

The body is the wing: one continuous surface from the nose to the wingtips.

Safety

Five independent layers

If one system fails, the next one is there.

  1. Layer oneBackup batteriesReserve power kept apart from the primary supply, so the aircraft always has enough to land.
  2. Layer twoCold gas thrustersCompressed-gas thrusters that steady the aircraft in an emergency.
  3. Layer threeCollision avoidanceSensors watch the airspace around the aircraft, all the time.
  4. Layer fourAutomated fire safetyDetection and suppression that act on their own, without waiting for a person.
  5. Layer fiveBallistic parachuteA whole-aircraft parachute, fired by a ballistic charge. The last layer.
