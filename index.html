Safety

Redundancy, layer by layer

Five layers betweenyou and the ground.

  1. Backup batteries

    Reserve power kept apart from the primary supply, so the aircraft always has enough to land.

  2. Cold gas thrusters

    Compressed-gas thrusters that steady the aircraft in an emergency.

  3. Collision avoidance

    Sensors watch the airspace around the aircraft, all the time.

  4. Automated fire safety

    Detection and suppression that act on their own, without waiting for a person.

  5. Ballistic parachute

    A whole-aircraft parachute, fired by a ballistic charge. The last layer.
